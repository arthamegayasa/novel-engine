# Chapter 1 plan: Clock

> Written before drafting. One screen. If this plan can't be filled, the chapter isn't ready to write.

- **Scene goal** (POV wants): Find out where she is and whether anyone from the hospital is here. Specifically: whether anyone from the fourth floor (her eleven beds) is here.
- **Why now** (what in the previous chapter forces this): Ch 0 ends at 02:48:04 with the building coming down. She has no interval; the first thing after the quake is frozen mud. (Ch 0 is not yet drafted; this plan takes the outline's ch 0 row and the bible's ruler as given.)
- **Obstacle / opposition**: The tear and the cold; a language she does not have until Oske binds her tongue; six strangers from other floors who are being handled by men with swords while she is handled separately; the Shoring's intake, which answers none of her questions; the swords themselves, which take for six and not for her.
- **Turn** (value at start → value at end): one survivor among seven (alive, and not alone) → the only one of seven without (grainless; named Clock; entered below the line). Outcome shape: Yes, but. Six from the hospital are here; none from her ward; and she is the odd one.
- **Ending hook** (decision; soft close after ch 0's cliff). Last beat: across the fire a Shoring man says "Clock" to another man, not to her, and she looks up anyway. The name has already taken where the sword did not.
- **Clues**:
  - plants: **C-002** (the name "Eightbreath", heard from a Shoring man at the fire; camouflage: name + her confident wrong guess that it is a breathing-stance name, by way of the sixth-floor physio who counts before a transfer); **C-019** (Oske enters the six under a heading, draws a line, enters her below it; she asks what the heading says; the Warden: "A clerical matter"; camouflage: plenty-of-nothing, inside the naming business right after the laugh); **C-021** (the intake: "On my right. Keep talking; the tear's loud"; he counts to eight under his breath before lifting her; camouflage: tic inside the intake chaos, and she approves of it as what you do with the concussed); **RH-8** (new, red-herring, her own inference: "seven of us for six swords; I was the one past the end of the row"; literally true and the wrong explanation; dies with RH-6 at ch 13).
  - echoes: none available (ch 0 not drafted). Motif touches: M-ice (the mud has ice in it; the sword's frost does not go to water in her hand), M-pen (her right hand is empty and closed as if around something).
  - payoffs: none. red herrings: RH-8 (above).
  - hidden hand on stage: the Warden runs the intake and names nothing about himself. He is the one who had six swords set and a fire already burning.
- **Continuity facts in play** (from `continuity.md`): none recorded yet. Facts this chapter will establish: the tear's sound and cold; the six and what they wore; arrivals come clothed as they died but carry nothing; Oske binds at the tear; six swords for this cast; the Warden's intake; Raras named Clock; the register heading and the line; "past the noon sag" is the Eaves hour; the Post is a day's walk.
- **Motifs to touch**: ice water (cold at the tear; frost on the blade); eight breaths (once, under his breath, inside the lift); the register (first words; a heading she cannot read); the pen (the empty hand).
- **Emotional register**: inventory under shock. She counts people, floors, swords, degrees of cold. The one place it breaks: when he says keep talking and she gets to bed 8 and the sentence ends.
- **Rest or pressure chapter?** Pressure (arrival), with a comic beat at the fire (the ambulance driver's first words, transcribed by Oske with care).
- **Target words**: 2,500 (band 2,200 to 2,800).
- **Scene list**:
  1. **The tear, flat grey light.** Present: Raras on the frozen mud past the end of a row of six planted swords; the six kneeling with a hand each on a hilt (Pak Darto, Mbak Rini, a radiographer, the canteen cook, an ambulance driver, a visitor in a batik shirt and bathroom slippers); Shoring men coming out of the leaning pines; the Warden; Oske with a book. What happens: she counts; Rini recognises her face from the lifts and says the hospital's name aloud; nobody from the fourth floor; the men speak a language she does not have; the Warden comes to her, not to the six; she asks "Jam berapa?"; Oske binds her tongue (thumb, ink, a long look); the next sentence begins as noise and ends as words. What is withheld: who the Warden is; why he came to her first; why there was a fire already.
  2. **The intake.** On his right, keep talking, the count of eight, lifted. She talks because she is told to: the time (02:48), the lead on bed 7, the discharge note, Pak Yunus going home Tuesday, bed 8 (the sentence stops). Her teeth make talking hard; she counts instead. The six draw their swords out of the ground; frost on the blades goes to water in their hands. She has nothing in her hand. A Shoring man's sword is tried on her: the frost stays; her palm sticks to it; she has to peel it off. "Six set. Six took." She does the sum herself (RH-8). What is withheld: what "took" means; what she is.
  3. **The fire, dusk that is not dusk.** Oske reads first words back for the register, with care (the driver's are a swear; the men like it; the six do not laugh). "You said, and I quote, 'jam brapa'." She glosses it. "Clock," from a Shoring man. The Warden: "Clock. Noted." The six under a heading; a line; her below it; "A clerical matter." She hears "Eightbreath" and places it (wrongly). Someone says the hour is past the noon sag; she had it as evening. Last beat: "Clock," across the fire, not to her.
- **The sentence the chapter exists to earn**: "Six set. Six took." (and she, seven, counting herself last).
- **What the reader should believe at the end that is not true**: that she has no grain because there were seven of them for six swords and she landed past the end of the row; that the Warden's handling of her is the Eaves' standard procedure for the concussed; that "Eightbreath" is a breathing-stance name.
