# Outline: Not Yet

> Arc-level and chapter-level plan. Chapter rows are provisional until written; once a chapter is drafted, its row records what actually happened. Keep rows short. Detail goes into the chapter plan at draft time.

## Spine

- **Opening image** (ch 0): 02:47:04. A pen in her hand, a discharge note half written, the bradycardia alarm on bed 7, and she gets up to fix the lead.
- **Inciting incident** (ch 1): she lands at the tear on nothing. Six others land on swords.
- **First threshold** (ch 6): she dies, returns to the minute, dies in the quake, and arrives to find the Eaves did not reset. A season has passed. Wenna has a grave. A stranger says "Dok."
- **Midpoint reversal** (ch 17): Sekar wakes and repeats what Raras said to her in the tent. Nobody else was there. The source of their knowledge is Raras's own mouth. She refuses the extension and chooses a death.
- **Dark night** (ch 26): Dimas, twelve, with a sword, singing her lullaby with her wrong words. Tallow: "You're not the catch. You're the hook."
- **Climax** (ch 28): the Low Field. The Warden pinned and locked under the sky; Tris's grain holds the edge off her for eight breaths; she completes the determination; the hook comes out.
- **Final image** (ch 30): forty Eaves-years on, Tris, grainless, sitting with an unconscious stayer in a timber ward, talking.

## Cadence rules for this book

- **Cliffhanger pattern**: hard hooks (cliff) at ch 0, 11, 23, 28 only; each honoured in the next chapter's opening, never cheated. Cycle-reset hooks at 5, 12, 19, 25 (each the dry ordinal line). Soft close after every hard one (1, 12, 24, 29).
- **Hook rotation**: no type three times running; no more than two consecutive identical. Sequence (ch 0 to 30): cliff, reveal, quiet dread, threat, comic, cycle-reset, reveal, quiet dread, decision, threat, quiet dread, cliff, cycle-reset, reveal, threat, quiet dread, comic, decision, threat, cycle-reset, threat, decision, quiet dread, cliff, reveal, cycle-reset, reveal, decision, cliff, quiet dread, none. Minute chapters do not all end on reveal (20 ends on threat).
- **Ruler schedule** (R1 = bed 7 alarm and the lead; R2 = Yunus's question and "Belum, Pak"): ch 0 kept/kept; ch 6 kept/kept; ch 13 kept/kept (linen room between the lead and bed 3); ch 20 broken/kept-in-words; ch 26 kept/kept; ch 29 broken/broken.
- **Rest chapters**: 4, 16, 22. Each shifts one value and carries one plant that pays within ten chapters and one that pays later (4: Wenna's hand → 8; the bell → 11. 16: the tradition → 26; the humming → 26. 22: the lamp → 27; the second volume → 30).
- **POV interludes**: ch 15 and ch 30, close third, Tris. Marked in the chapter frontmatter. No other POV.
- **Minute chapters** (6, 13, 20, 26, 29): the minute is the first scene, ~500 to 700 words, the fixed ruler run as planned (unchanged / partial break / straight / full break); the quake is one flat sentence; the arrival is the second scene and carries the turn. Ch 29 is the minute alone.
- **Opening types** rotated per the title cell; no type three times running.
- **Arc length band**: 10 to 11 chapters. Cycle lengths 6, 7, 8, 6, 3.
- **Outcome distribution** per arc: roughly half No-and-furthermore (3, 5, 11, 12, 19, 23, 25, 28), a third Yes-but (2, 4, 8, 10, 14, 21, 22, 27), a few No (7, 18), clean Yes only at 9, 20, 24 (payoffs) and 16 (rest).
